Teacher plan · 25 minutes

Class: Grade 7 · Unit 2: Awesome animals · Lesson 5, part 1 (Vocabulary and Grammar)
Lesson aims: to learn verbs associated with animal actions; to learn how to use the first conditional.
Target language: bite, sting, roar, fight, dive, dig, hide, lay eggs, curl up, fly away, run away, die.
Materials: Unit 2 flashcards (animal verbs), grammar animation, traffic light cards, device or projector, notebooks.

Differentiation

  • Support 🌱: first-letter hints in Look and Match, only the six words of the round on the board in Fly Swat Battle, sentence frames in Garden Helpers.
  • Standard 🌿: full activities.
  • Challenge 🌳: no hints, all twelve Lesson 1 words on the board, and three written sentences. Extra: make a sentence with unless or think of the result of each action.

Notes and keys

  • Fly Swat Battle: in class, use a real fly swat and the words on the board; the game also has a “Show a clue” button (picture instead of sound) for deaf/hard of hearing students.
  • Verb pictures are emoji scenes chosen for the game. Compare them with the Class Book pictures (2.10) and the numbers in your Teacher’s Book (page 105–106).
  • Verbs found in the folk tale: fighting, roared, hiding, bite, dive, lay (eggs), curled (up) = 7.
  • Rules: if + present simple; will + infinitive; we use it for real and possible situations in the future.
  • Deaf/hard of hearing students: use flashcards, gestures and written examples; audio is optional.
  • Extra activities in the Teacher’s Book for this lesson: Photocopiables 2, 7 and 11.
